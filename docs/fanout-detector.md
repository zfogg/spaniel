# Fan-out amplification

The fan-out detector reports a potential investigation when one observed parent has at least five outbound CLIENT or PRODUCER children across at least three dependency/operation groups. It requires a named dependency and operation, an observed parent, valid timing, and at least some time inside the parent. Database spans are excluded in favor of N+1; groups of five or more identical calls are excluded to limit overlap with chatty HTTP. Retransmitted span IDs and server counterparts do not count. Traces marked partial and traces above 10,000 spans are skipped.

The fingerprint lists up to eight groups and their counts, one example child is linked, and the count covers eligible calls. Coverage is the union of child intervals clipped to the parent; overlap is the sum of clipped durations minus that union. These are observed timing, not critical-path attribution or potential savings, so wasted time is zero. Instrumentation that emits distinct duplicate client spans cannot reliably be distinguished from real parallel calls. Findings update when later spans arrive, but the existing detector persistence does not retract a finding if later evidence changes its classification.
